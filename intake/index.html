Ultra Training Intake

Everything needed to build a personalised endurance plan — and to know when the honest answer is “see a doctor first.” Answer what you can; the last section is for everything we forgot to ask. Two answers matter more than the rest: your name, which is how your plan is kept separate from everyone else's, and which edition of the race — the year sets how long the plan is, and therefore what shape it takes.

How to use it. Sections marked core are the minimum for a safe, useful plan — answer those first. deeper sections sharpen it. Your answers save in this browser as you type. First, so everything that follows is filed against you and nobody else. Your name becomes your own folder — your profile, your log, your plan. Nothing you write here can reach another runner's training, and nothing of theirs reaches yours.

core
Stop rule. If you answer yes to any of the next four questions, the right next step is a doctor, not a training plan. Endurance training is a real cardiac load and a plan built over an unexamined symptom is worse than no plan.
